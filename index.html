Selected work

The problems I’m proudest of solving at Kuhoo Finance, and the numbers that moved. Plus one production incident worth learning from.

Decision engine · Green Channel

From 2.5 hours to 34 minutes

I own the decision engine that has underwritten 2.75 lakh loans. Green Channel is its fast track for the applications the data already says yes to.

Problem
Every application waited in the same manual review queue, so strong applicants waited as long as everyone else, about 2.5 hours.
What I built
Green Channel, a fast-track flow. Data-driven eligibility checks decide which applications can skip the manual queue; the rest go through standard review.
Result
Turnaround fell 77% to 34 minutes and disbursement conversion rose 8%. Faster decisions turned into more funded loans.

Loan calculations · ₹300 Cr book

Every rupee of a ₹300 Cr book, calculated

I’m the individual contributor for all loan calculations at Kuhoo Finance.

Problem
Lending math has to be exact for every loan, every month. EMIs, part payments that reshape a schedule and foreclosures that close a loan early all have to agree to the rupee.
What I built
All of the platform’s loan calculations, including the EMI calculator (designed and built) and part payment and foreclosure, end to end.
Result
These calculations power Kuhoo Finance’s ₹300 Cr loan book.

Lending platform · Architecture

One platform, 4–5 lenders

The origination, servicing and lender-routing platform behind our education loans.

Problem
Every NBFC lender brings its own rules for checks, sanction and disbursement, and education loans pay out in tranches instead of all at once.
What I built
The LOS, the LMS and a multi-lender aggregator, with KYC, Video KYC, CKYC, e-sign and tranche disbursement as shared flows that every lender plugs into. I also designed and integrated the OneFin loan management system.
Result
500+ applications a day go from sanction to disbursement automatically, across 8+ integrated services, with 70% less manual document work. I also led the ₹50 Cr portfolio migration from a partner NBFC to Kuhoo Finance.

KYC + OCR · Automation

72% of documents never reach a human

OCR and an AI voice bot took the repetitive work out of loan operations.

Problem
Operations teams checked PAN cards, Aadhaar, salary slips and mark sheets by hand, and spent much of the day calling applicants.
What I built
A re-architected verification microservice on HyperVerge, Digio and Signzy, covering e-Aadhaar via DigiLocker, PAN, Aadhaar and video KYC. On top of it, OCR for those four document types and an AI voice bot that together handle 80K+ events a month.
Result
72% of documents skip manual review, and manual calling effort is down 60%.

DPDP Act · Compliance

Privacy built into every loan

Data protection for India’s DPDP Act, designed into the lending platform from the schema up.

Problem
India’s DPDP Act raised the bar for how lenders store and account for personal data, and tracing what happened to a loan meant digging through records by hand.
What I built
An audit log of the entire loan history, object-level authorization on customer and loan APIs, role-based field-level security across every backend API, and a PII vault with lookups on a hash index.
Result
No more manual tracing, and no path for one customer to read another customer’s data or loans.
Postmortem Production · Celery workers → AWS ElastiCache Resolved

The Redis connection leak that came from gevent

Symptom
Open Redis connections on AWS ElastiCache kept climbing in production.
Root cause
gevent context switching inside the Celery workers left connections open.
My role
Led the root-cause analysis and shipped the fix.
Takeaway
Changing a worker’s concurrency model changes how every pooled client behaves. Test connection handling under the pool you actually run in production.

About

I’m a backend engineer in Mumbai. I build the systems behind digital lending, the ones that verify who you are, decide on your loan and send the money exactly once.

I started in 2020 building Django APIs and secure internal tools. In 2022 I moved into fintech, and lending has been my focus ever since. Today I lead a team of 6 developers on our loan origination and loan management systems, and I own the decision engine, the payment service and the loan calculations behind a ₹300 Cr book.

As tech lead I run requirement elicitation, HLD/LLD and sprint planning, review designs and code, sit on the Architecture Review Board and help hire. I’ve mentored interns into backend developers and built our onboarding process for new engineers. I use Claude Code and Cursor every day, and specs and tests keep them honest.

How I work

  • Retries should be boring.

    Every endpoint that moves money takes an idempotency key, so a double tap, a timeout or a redelivered message can’t pay out twice.

  • Spec first, then agents.

    Features start as a written spec. Claude Code and Cursor implement against it, pytest proves it, and CI blocks the merge until the tests pass.

  • Privacy belongs in the data model.

    PII sits in a vault, access is checked per object, and every change to a loan is written to an audit log.

  • Find the root cause.

    Production issues get a proper root-cause analysis. That’s how a Redis connection leak got traced to gevent context switching inside Celery workers.
